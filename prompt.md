# The commission

> lets do tetris

Eleventh game in the series, and the first deliberately *simple* one: the
whole juice budget lands on a single screen instead of a whole world.
Same method — one owner builds a single-file tribute, deterministic
`?shot=` captures, four harsh sub-agent critics (composition, game-feel,
HUD, and a Tetris-fidelity judge), honest scores, publish. The proof
harness's next species: **stacks as theorems** — a bot that must survive
on every seed, controls that must top out, and mechanism proofs for the
7-bag, SRS wall kicks, T-spins, lock delay and the scoring table.
